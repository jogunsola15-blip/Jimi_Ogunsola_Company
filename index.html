About Us

Welcome to Jimi Ogunsola & Co.. We are a firm of Estate Surveying and Valuation duly registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) and the Estate Surveyors and Valuation Registration Board of Nigeria (ESVARBON) to practise the profession of estate surveying and valuation throughout Nigeria. We are experts in the field of Asset Valuation, Property/Facility Management, Property Sales Agency, Property Documentation, Feasibility and Viability Studies, etc. We deliver our services with professionalism, diligence and Integrity.
